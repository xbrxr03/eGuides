The Other Dial

Should I change the model, or the effort?

Effort controls how much work Claude is willing to do on a given problem. The model controls Claude's fixed capabilities — what it's able to do at all.

→ Want Claude to work more thoroughly and carefully through this problem? Raise the effort.

→ Problem is especially difficult and needs more capability than the current model has? Change the model.

How they interact:

CombinationFeels like
Sonnet at high effortSpending an afternoon with a generalist
Opus at low effortSpending five minutes with an expert
Fable at any effortWorking with a specialist who spots solutions everyone else misses

In Claude Code, both live behind one command:

/model

Opens a picker for the model itself (Opus 5 / Fable 5 / Sonnet 5 / more), plus an Effort submenu — low, medium, high (default), xhigh ("Extra" in the picker), max.
